Guided visualization

Through guided mental imagery, you work on patterns, reduce stress, and strengthen your clarity to make decisions. Ideal if your mind feels saturated or on autopilot. In your first session we define your goal from the start: what you want to achieve, what's holding you back, and what the next step is.

How a session works, step by step

What will happen in your first session

1. Before

You book via WhatsApp and receive confirmation of your appointment. If your session is online, you confirm your payment by transfer and I'll send you the details.

2. During

It's 45 minutes. We start by talking about what you want to work on, continue with a guided visualization, and close with a clear idea to take with you.

3. After

You leave with greater clarity and calm, and with a defined next step. If you'd like to continue your process, we'll schedule your next session at a time that works for you.

Your space is confidential

A safe, confidential space

Our approach is oriented toward future possibilities, setting aside past events that may have affected your present state. You'll gain effective, positive, and rewarding tools.
